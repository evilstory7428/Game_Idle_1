# Pixel prototype — 2026-09-24

Current art is procedural Canvas pixel art. No raster images are loaded or used in the game or menus. Previous HD assets remain archived in assets/ but are inactive.

The original western defense concept is restored visually: female starter with long dark hair, cowboy hat, vest, jeans and boots; five left-side defenders; enemies entering from the right. Tank carries a mace and shield, melee fighter a saber, support a medical bag, demolition hero dynamite. Ranged heroes face right.

Background sequence: animals and cactus frontier (1–100), cavemen and rocky canyon (101–200), zombies and graveyard (201–300), then repeat. The defended wooden western village stays on the left. Bosses use larger bodies and gold markers. Menus reuse the same pixel drawing functions on canvas thumbnails.
